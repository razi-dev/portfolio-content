Stepped into quantum computing at Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 × QuantumX – Quantum Hack Day, hosted at TinkerSpace Kochi on November 1, 2026. With almost no prior quantum experience, the journey started from the fundamentals - qubits, quantum circuits and quantum algorithms - and moved quickly toward a practical build.

Collaborating with a multidisciplinary team, designed and built Q-DisC: a hybrid quantum-classical machine learning pipeline and clinical dashboard that classifies Alzheimer's Disease and Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI) from EEG spectral biomarkers. The stack combines Qiskit and Qiskit Machine Learning, a Variational Quantum Classifier (VQC), ZZFeatureMap quantum feature maps, PCA for dimensionality reduction, and classical benchmarking with SVM and Random Forest.

An experimental learning project rather than a claim of quantum advantage - and a first step into an entirely new domain.
