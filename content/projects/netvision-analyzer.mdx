Cellular signal strength fluctuates dramatically based on topology, urban canyon effects, and base station handovers. NetVision captures raw mobile radio telemetry directly from device hardware and correlates it with geospatial cellular tower registries. By querying localized base station coordinates from MongoDB spatial databases and computing signal gradients, NetVision renders intuitive coverage maps that illuminate coverage quality across 4G LTE and 5G spectrum bands.
