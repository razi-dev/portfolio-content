TrueTalkie addresses one of the fundamental vulnerabilities of modern voice communications: the total reliance on centralized cellular base stations and public internet infrastructure. In disaster-recovery scenarios, remote fieldwork, and congested environments, conventional VoIP apps fail immediately upon link degradation. TrueTalkie introduces a resilient hybrid communication topology. When internet access is present, audio streams navigate low-latency WebRTC peer connections coordinated via Supabase Realtime signaling. The instant internet connectivity drops, the system seamlessly transitions into a zero-configuration ad-hoc local mesh, utilizing raw UDP multicast packets broadcasted directly over local Wi-Fi or mobile hotspot subnets.
